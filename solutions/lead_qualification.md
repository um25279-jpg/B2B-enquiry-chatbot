# Lead Qualification

The chatbot uses the FIT-NAT framework to understand and qualify potential customers.

- **Fit:** Does the customer's business match the company's target customers?
- **Need:** What products or services does the customer require?
- **Authority:** Is the customer involved in the purchasing decision?
- **Timing:** When does the customer plan to make the purchase?
- **Ability:** Does the customer have the budget and ability to proceed?

The chatbot gathers this information through conversational follow-up questions.
